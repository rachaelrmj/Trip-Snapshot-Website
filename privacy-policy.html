Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2/16/2026

Trip Snapshot respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any information you provide while using the website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data

Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Trip details such as destination, dates, and preferences
  • Account information (such as name or email if account features are used)
  • Saved itineraries and planning inputs

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and interactions with the site
  • Device or browser information used to improve performance and usability

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Generate personalized travel recommendations and itineraries
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the platform
  • Understand how users interact with features to guide improvements

Your information is used solely to support the planning experience.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and store data securely within the platform. While we strive to safeguard your data, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Trip Snapshot may use external APIs or services to provide travel recommendations, mapping, weather information, or related features. These services may process limited data necessary to deliver results.

We do not sell or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Tracking

The website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Maintain session functionality
  • Improve performance
  • Understand usage patterns

You can adjust your browser settings to manage or disable cookies.

Your Choices

You may choose to:

  • Limit the information you provide
  • Delete saved itineraries or account data (where available)
  • Stop using the service at any time

Children's Privacy

Trip Snapshot is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements or changes to the platform. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the site administrator through the provided contact method.

Summary

Trip Snapshot is designed to help you plan travel with confidence while respecting your privacy. We collect only the information necessary to provide planning features and continuously work to maintain a safe and transparent experience.
