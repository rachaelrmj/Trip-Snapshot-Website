Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Trip Snapshot?

Trip Snapshot is a travel planning website that helps you create personalized trip plans based on your destination, dates, and preferences. It brings recommendations and itinerary planning into one convenient place to make the process faster and less stressful.

Why was Trip Snapshot created?

The site was created to address the common frustration of planning trips across multiple websites. It aims to reduce overwhelm by centralizing information and providing clear, personalized guidance.

How does Trip Snapshot work?

Simply enter your trip details — such as destination, travel dates, and interests — and the site generates suggested activities, recommendations, and a structured itinerary to guide your planning.

Do I need to create an account to use the site?

You can explore trip planning features without an account, but creating an account allows you to save itineraries and access them later.

Can I book flights or hotels through Trip Snapshot?

No. Trip Snapshot focuses on helping you plan and organize your trip. It does not handle bookings, payments, or real-time reservations.

How are recommendations generated?

Recommendations are based on the preferences and trip details you provide, along with travel data sources and APIs that help surface relevant attractions, activities, and travel ideas.

Can I customize my itinerary?

Yes. You can adjust your preferences and regenerate your itinerary to better match your travel style and interests.

Is Trip Snapshot free to use?

Yes. The platform is designed as a planning tool and is free to explore.

Who is Trip Snapshot designed for?

It's designed for anyone planning a trip, including solo travelers, couples, families, groups, and budget-conscious explorers who want a simpler way to organize their plans.

Will my saved itineraries be stored securely?

We aim to handle user data responsibly and store saved itineraries securely within the platform for easy access.

What if I can't find the information I need?

Trip Snapshot is intended to guide your planning and provide inspiration. You may still choose to verify details or explore additional sources as you finalize your trip.

Does the site provide real-time pricing or availability?

No. Real-time pricing and availability are not included. The focus is on helping you plan rather than manage bookings.

Can I access the site on mobile devices?

Yes. The site is designed to be responsive so you can plan your trip on desktop, tablet, or mobile.

Does the site provide real-time pricing or availability?

No. Real-time pricing and availability are not included. The focus is on helping you plan rather than manage bookings.
